Features

Everything You Need to Run Your Venue

Eventikk brings together all the tools a modern event business needs — in one clean, powerful platform.

Smart Booking Management

Handle single and multi-hall bookings with real-time conflict detection, calendar view, and auto-confirmations.

  • Visual calendar with drag & drop
  • Booking approval workflows
  • Automated reminders

Multi-Hall Management

Manage unlimited halls, set capacities, amenities, and pricing per hall — all from a single account.

  • Unlimited hall configurations
  • Per-hall pricing rules
  • Real-time availability

Billing & Invoicing

Generate professional invoices, track payments, and manage advance deposits with full audit trails.

  • Auto-generated invoices
  • Advance & installment tracking
  • PDF export & print

Staff & Role Management

Assign granular permissions per role — managers, cashiers, receptionists — with full activity logs.

  • Custom role creation
  • Permission-based access
  • Staff activity tracking

Analytics & Reports

Get detailed insights on revenue, bookings, popular dates, and hall utilization with visual charts.

  • Revenue dashboards
  • Booking trend analysis
  • Exportable reports

Multi-Tenant SaaS

Each business gets its own isolated workspace. Perfect for chains, franchises, or resellers.

  • Isolated tenant data
  • Custom subdomain
  • White-label ready

Everything you need to know about Eventikk — Pakistan's leading venue ERP.

What is Eventikk?

Eventikk — also commonly searched as Eventik or Eventick — is Pakistan's #1 ERP platform for marquee halls, catering businesses, and event venues. It helps venue owners manage bookings, billing, staff, and clients from one smart dashboard.

Is Eventikk free to try?

Yes! Eventikk offers a free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required. Paid plans start from PKR 7,499/month.

Who should use Eventikk?

Eventikk is built for marquee hall owners, banquet venue managers, catering businesses, and event planners across Pakistan who want to replace registers and WhatsApp with a professional system.
