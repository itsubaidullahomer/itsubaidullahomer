Built for Pakistan's Event Industry

Pakistan's marquee industry was drowning in registers, WhatsApp chaos, and lost revenue. We didn't just see the problem — we eliminated it. Eventikk is the all-in-one ERP that turns every venue into a professionally run operation: bookings, billing, staff, and reporting — all in one place.

Our Mission

Digitizing Pakistan's ₨50B Event Industry

Pakistan has thousands of marquee halls, banquet venues, and catering businesses. Most of them still manage bookings in registers, invoices on paper, and staff over WhatsApp.

Eventikk was founded to change that — to give every venue owner the same powerful tools that large hotel chains use, at a price that makes sense for local businesses.
