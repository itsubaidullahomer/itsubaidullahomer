## Why this project is here

Ali Foodies is a small, straightforward website rebuild. I included it because the attention I give a product should carry through to the smaller projects too.

My work here covers product design, UX, and development. Each decision has a reason: what customers need to find, what they should see first, and how easily they can take the next step.

## The decisions behind the design

Almost every customer visits on a phone, so I started with that experience.

- **Put ordering first.** A clear action on the opening screen helps customers get straight to their order.
- **Make the menu easy to explore.** Categories, food photos, visible prices, and clear buttons help people choose on a small screen.
- **Keep practical details close.** Hours, location, directions, and contact options answer the questions customers have before visiting.

## What I built

A mobile-first redesign and rebuild, carrying those choices from the layout into the working interface. The comparison shows the actual previous site alongside the new preview, so you can explore the details for yourself.
