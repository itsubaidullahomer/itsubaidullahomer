About Us

Appkeeper Pte. Ltd.

Appkeeper is an AI-first software engineering company based in Singapore. Since 2023, we've built production software for companies that want AI inside real workflows — analytics, internal operations, customer portals, infrastructure monitoring, and automation. We usually start with the core platform: data connectors, dashboards, APIs, permissions, billing, monitoring, and CI/CD. Then we add AI where it genuinely makes the product better — natural-language analytics, autonomous dashboard creation, workflow agents, support automation, and AI-assisted incident triage.
