      We are looking for a Full Stack Engineer to join our dynamic team.
      We build scalable web applications using React/NodeJS/PostgreSQL. Our core technologies are Next.js and Supabase.
      The ideal candidate has a strong foundation of backend logic & databases and has production experience.
      You will be working directly with clients abroad on exciting cutting edge products.

      Who we are
      AZKY Tech Labs is a UK/Dubai based agency that specializes in building AI powered solutions (SaaS, Internal tools etc) for clients around the world. Over the past 5 years, we have worked with more than 100 foreign companies (startups, SMEs and Enterprises) in USA, UK, EU and Australia.

      Who You Are
      You're a good fit if you have strong foundations and production experience in React/Node/PostgreSQL.
      • Availability: This role is for immediate hiring. It is not suitable for candidates who are currently employed full-time in another role.
      • Primary Interest: You have a strong, demonstrable passion for backend development. Your primary interest must be in server-side logic, APIs, and databases, not just UI development.
      • Technical Foundation: You must have solid experience with React.js, Node.js (ideally building REST APIs, e.g., with Express), Relational Databases (e.g., PostgreSQL or MySQL). You have architected databases from scratch.
      • Core Concepts: You have a strong grasp of Data Structures, Algorithms, and Client-Server Architecture.


      Key Responsibilities
      • Design, develop, and maintain web and mobile applications
      • Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code following industry best practices.
      • Collaborate with QA, and product teams to deliver robust features.
      • Optimize application performance, scalability, and security.
      • Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve technical issues across the stack.
      • Participate in code reviews, provide feedback, and ensure code quality.
      • Stay updated with emerging technologies and frameworks to enhance development efficiency.


      Soft skills
      • Good English
      • Strong attention to detail
      • Proactive, independent working style
      • Strong communication and teamwork skills
      • Effective time management and ability to prioritize tasks
      • Desire to learn and grow as a developer


      Salary Range: Competitive

      Perks for you
      • Quarterly Performance Bonus- based on performance and client outcomes
      • Company equipment (up to PKR 130k allowance to upgrade workstation)
      • Laptop allowance (when you use your own laptop)
      • Internet backup
      • Medical insurance (dependents and parents included)
      • Direct communication with clients and PMs (US, UK, EU) on a regular basis
      • Paid time off
      • Fully remote and open work culture

        Entry level

        Full-time

        IT Services and IT Consulting
