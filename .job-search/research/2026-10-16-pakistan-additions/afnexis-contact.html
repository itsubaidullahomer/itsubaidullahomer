What does Afnexis build?

Custom AI agents and the software around them: machine learning models, NLP systems, document processing, and the integrations that connect them to your tools. 50+ projects delivered for 30+ clients.

How do I get started with my project?

Fill out the form on this page, email info@afnexis.com, or call +1 646-379-7856. We reply within one US business day. Most clients start with the $1,500 workflow audit.

What does it cost?

Most custom agents: $12k to $45k, fixed price. Start with a $1,500 workflow audit, credited toward the build. Fixed price, paid in three milestones: 40% to start, 30% at the agreed midpoint, 30% at delivery. Each milestone has written acceptance criteria.

Every project comes with a 30 day bug-fix warranty after delivery. During the build you get weekly updates, a shared Slack channel, and a demo at the end of each sprint.
