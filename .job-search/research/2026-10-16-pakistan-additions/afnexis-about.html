What we believe

Six rules. Not posters on a wall. Actual operating principles.

These are how we make decisions: what to build, how to build it, and when to push back on a client's request.

01

Ship what works

We test every tool against one question: does it solve this client's problem better than what we already use?

02

Your team, not ours

We join your Slack, show up to your standups, and work in your timezone when it matters.

03

Done means in production

A feature isn't done until it's live, monitored, and the client confirms it works.

04

Engineers own everything

Our engineers work from database schema to deploy pipeline without handoffs.

05

Two-week cycles

Every engagement runs in two-week sprints with working software at the end of each.

06

Compliance from day one

Privacy and security controls go into the architecture on day one, not added before an audit.
