How we work

We keep teams small and senior, favor direct delivery over process, and give people room to do work they can stand behind.

Senior ownership

You own real systems end to end — from architecture decisions through production behavior.

Durable engineering

Hiring process

A short, honest path from application to offer.

No endless loops. We keep it focused, grounded in real work, and respectful of your time.

01

Application

Send your resume and a short note. We read every application and respond either way.

02

Intro conversation

A focused call about your experience, how you work, and the systems you have built.

03

Technical deep dive

A practical, hands-on session grounded in real problems rather than trick puzzles.

04

Offer

A clear offer, a direct answer on scope and level, and a straightforward path to start.
