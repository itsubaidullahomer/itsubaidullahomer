Mid-Level Full Stack Developer (2+ Years in MERN Stack)

Ready to build sleek, scalable full-stack products with React, Node.js, and a touch of Next.js magic?

Job Responsibilities

We're on the hunt for a Mid-Level Full Stack Developer who’s passionate about crafting smooth user experiences and robust backend systems. If you thrive in a fast-moving environment, love solving real world problems, and enjoy collaborating across teams to ship high-impact features, we want to hear from you! Join us to help shape a platform users love, push the boundaries of what’s possible, and grow your skillset alongside a sharp, fun-loving team.

  • Develop and enhance user-facing features using React (and preferably Next.js).
  • Build reusable components and libraries to streamline future development.
  • Design, implement, and maintain RESTful and GraphQL APIs using Node.js (Nest.js preferred).
  • Write efficient SQL/NoSQL queries for data integrity, reporting, and analytics.
  • Identify and troubleshoot performance issues and infrastructure errors.
  • Collaborate closely with frontend, QA, and product teams to deliver high-quality solutions.
  • Contribute to system architecture and help design for performance, reliability, and scalability.
  • Work with third-party services and APIs including Stripe, PayPal, Twilio, SendGrid, and others.

Job Requirements

  • 2+ years of hands-on experience with the MERN stack in a production environment.
  • Strong experience with React (Next.js experience is a big plus).
  • Proficiency in Node.js, ideally with experience in NestJS for backend API development.
  • Experience with MongoDB or similar NoSQL databases.
  • Familiarity with frontend UI libraries such as Material UI, Semantic UI, or Ant Design.
  • Solid understanding of state management tools like Redux or React Context.
  • Experience integrating and maintaining third-party APIs.
  • A strong problem-solver with a proactive, self-starting mindset.
