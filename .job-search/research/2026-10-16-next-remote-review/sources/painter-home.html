Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do you typically see results?

We are almost always able to generate a positive ROI starting in month 1!

Is marketing for painters different?

Marketing for painters is unique. Painting is typically a want, a luxury item. As such, marketing must be specially tailored to have maximum effect on your ideal customer.

How many new painting leads will I get?

The answer to this question depends on a few factors, including your geographic area, target market, and ad budget. Though we can guarantee that you’ll get a steady flow of new leads, predicting the number of leads that will come in each day requires us to gather more information. Contact us for a free strategy call!

How much do your services cost?

You might have noticed that we don’t show our prices online. This is because each marketing package is custom-tailored for our clients. Pricing depends on your geographic region, with large, urban areas requiring more significant investment to outrank the increased levels of competition. We aim to make our services affordable, so please get in touch for a free strategy call and quote.

How do I know what marketing services I need?

You might be wondering if you need full-scale marketing execution or help in a single area (like getting to the top of the Google search results page). Often, painting contractors come to us knowing they need help with their marketing, but they don’t know where to start. During your free strategy call, we’ll do a deep dive into your current marketing activities and your overall goals to determine which services will help you achieve those objectives.
