## The problem

Around 99.9% of Ali Foodies’ visitors are on a phone. The existing website needed to work for that screen first.

## What I built

A mobile-first redesign and rebuild. The comparison shows the actual previous site alongside the new preview.

## The hard parts

Keep the menu and ordering path readable and easy to use on a small screen, then adapt the design for larger screens.

## Results

The new version is live as a preview at [alifoodiess.vercel.app](https://alifoodiess.vercel.app) while I finish it. This is ongoing work, not a completed launch.
