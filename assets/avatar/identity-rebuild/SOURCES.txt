Blender 5.2.2 LTS performs actual mesh deformation, fitting, material baking,
rigging and rendering. Scripts do not imply that a human sculptor operated the
mouse or that the result is a scan.

The initial landmark-informed sculpt used MediaPipe's estimated depth. Review
identified that as insufficiently camera-independent. The newer camera-fit
study uses only 2D observations with a separate perspective camera for each
reference, symmetric surface changes, bounded displacements and regularization.
Its reprojection residual is not an identity accuracy percentage. Eye/globe
contact is evaluated separately rather than altered by uncertain depth fitting.

The continuous eye surfaces are authored geometry. The skin uses a complete
CC0 UV albedo atlas with a warm pigment adjustment and local beard-root shading.
The facial hair consists of surface-conforming curves; export may bake these
into the body's UV maps to avoid excessive geometry and morph-target memory.
This is ordinary material baking, not a front-portrait illusion.

Hair candidates are independent studies. Elvaerwyn's Maxwell mesh carries
CC-BY attribution and is documented separately by the reviewer. Its source
license and required credit must accompany any distributed derived asset.

All character renders in this directory remain studies until an independent
visual review explicitly records acceptance. Technical tests do not constitute
approval of likeness, natural motion or suitability for the public website.

Fitted-head anatomy, grooming and body study
-----------------------------------------
The replacement head uses separate continuous eye globes with authored brown
iris pigmentation and portable PBR clearcoat. The original flat eye and mouth
closure faces are removed. Upper eyelashes and wetlines are real meshes,
reconstructed from the fitted lid boundary for every facial target. Teeth,
gum arches, palate, cavity and tongue are authored geometry; dental-v4 is the
current independently preferred oral refinement, not a dental scan.

Fifteen English speech shapes are authored geometric combinations of the
fitted head's facial channels, with a real tongueOut target on oral geometry.
These shapes alone do not imply automatic phoneme/audio alignment.

Facial eyebrows/moustache/chin hair are fine Blender curves rooted in actual
surface triangles. Photo27 provides observed placement through its solved
camera, not a photographic facial texture. The same triangle frames attach
the fibers to every fitted-head target. Skin and lip pigmentation is authored
vertex color. Browser PBR maps bake that pigmentation and the real fibers
into the head's UV layout; dense editable fibers remain in the source .blend.

The ear-breadth augmentation is an independently reviewed, local geometric
offset applied identically to the neutral and all facial targets. It preserves
the ear attachment boundary. Global facial dimensions have not been changed
to compensate for differences in camera, lighting or expression.

Body integration uses a documented uniform scale and translation derived from
the existing rig's eye spacing, not a measured biometric scale. Lower neck
vertices are fitted below the collar; facial topology and target ordering stay
intact. Source scalp/facial curves follow the head bone and facial targets.
The body retains 34 authored gesture/idle clips. The public hero still uses the
approved static image; none of these fitted-head studies is a published final.

The first re-compressed tangent export was rejected after visual QA found eye
position changes from repeated quantization. Final skin-study packing uses
lossless Meshopt encoding after quantization, with all-node world-position
comparison and decoded glTF validation. Technical validation is separate from
visual approval, which remains outstanding for the complete character.
